# Judge's Guide

A one-page guide for judges. You score on your own tablet, laptop or phone, in its web browser. There is nothing to install.

## Signing in

1. Connect your device to the event Wi-Fi the tabulator tells you to use. Do not use mobile data.
2. Open your browser (Chrome, Edge or Safari) and type the address on your access slip, for example `http://192.168.1.10:3000`.
3. Choose your name, type your **access code**, and press **Sign in**.

Keep your access code to yourself. Anyone who has it can enter scores as you.

You can be signed in on **one device at a time**. Use the same device for the whole event.

- *Judge 1 is already signed in on another device* — you, or someone else, is signed in as you elsewhere. Tell the tabulator.
- *This device cannot open the judge scoresheet* — the tabulator has limited scoring to registered devices and this is not one of them. Show the screen to the tabulator.
- *The tabulator disconnected this device* — sign in again if this is your scoresheet.

## Scoring

Each category you are judging has its own tab at the top: **Dance Showdown**, **Best Music**, **Festival Queen**. The number on a tab, such as `3/9`, is how many contingents you have fully scored.

- Each row is a contingent, in order of performance. Each column is a criterion.
- Type the points you give. The most you can give is shown under the criterion name, for example **max 35**.
- Decimals are allowed, up to two places: `32.5`, `18.25`.
- Press **Enter** or the **down arrow** to move to the contingent below. Press **Tab** to move to the next criterion.

Your scores save by themselves as you type. You can change any score until you submit.

On the right of each row you will see your **Total score** (the sum of the points you gave), any **Deduction** the tabulator has recorded, your **Final score** (total less deduction), and your **Rank** — how that contingent stands among the ones you have scored so far. The official result is decided by adding up every judge's ranks, so check that your ranks say what you mean. If two contingents end with the same final score they share the places between them: tied for 2nd and 3rd, each gets a rank of 2.5.

Open **Criteria guide** at the bottom of the sheet to read the full description of each criterion.

## What the colours mean

| You see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| A white box | The score is saved. |
| A yellow box | The score is being saved. It turns white in a moment. |
| A red box | That is not a valid score: it is over the maximum or not a number. Fix it. |
| **All scores saved** (top right) | Everything you typed is safely with the tabulator. |
| **Scores not saved — retrying** (top right, in red) | Your device has lost the connection. See below. |

## If you are asked to score on paper

The tabulator may hand you a printed scoresheet instead. Write the points for each criterion, up to the maximum printed under its name, and the total for each contingent. Leave deduction, final score and rank empty. Sign the sheet and write the date and time. The tabulating team enters your scores afterwards and gives you a printed copy to check and sign.

## If the connection drops

Keep the page open and keep scoring. Your device holds on to what you type and sends it as soon as the connection returns. Do not close the browser or refresh the page while the red message is showing — that is the only way scores can be lost. If it stays red for more than a minute, tell the tabulator.

## Submitting

When every contingent in a category is fully scored, **Submit final scores** at the bottom becomes available.

1. Review your scores and ranks.
2. Press **Submit final scores** and confirm.

Submitting locks that category. Do it once for each category you judge.

Made a mistake after submitting? Tell the tabulator. They can reopen your scoresheet; you then correct it and submit again.

## When you are done

Once every tab shows **Submitted**, press **Sign out**. You will be asked to sign the printed copy of your scoresheet.
